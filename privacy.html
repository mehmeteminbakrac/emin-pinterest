Privacy Policy

App: emin board reader
Owner: emin (pinterest.com/eminbakrac)
Last updated: 7 November 2026

What this app is

A private, single-user tool. It connects only to the owner's own Pinterest account to read the owner's boards and saved Pins for personal inspiration and reference. It is not offered to any other Pinterest user.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app reads the owner's own boards, board sections and Pins (titles, descriptions, links and image URLs). It does not access other users' data and does not create, edit or delete any content on Pinterest.

Use and storage

Data is stored only on the owner's personal computer and used solely by the owner. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Access tokens

Pinterest access tokens are kept locally on the owner's computer and never shared. Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest settings.

Deletion

All locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time. Revoking the app's access in Pinterest stops all further data access.
